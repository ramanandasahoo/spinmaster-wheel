          Help

          Spinning

          Press Spin. The wheel stops on the winner under the pointer at the top. With the Spin button selected, Enter or Space also works.

          Editing options

          Add options in the Options panel. Change a label or color directly in its row, and use the arrow buttons or the drag handle to reorder. Undo and Redo cover recent edits.

          Weighted mode

          Turn it on to give each option a weight from 1 to 100. A higher weight makes the segment larger and more likely to win.

          Saving

          Everything is saved on this device in your browser. Saved wheels can be exported as JSON to move them to another device.

          Installing

          Use your browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. After the first visit SpinMaster also works offline.

          SpinMaster runs entirely in your browser. Your wheels never leave this device.
